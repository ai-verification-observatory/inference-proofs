    Glyph

    Unforgeable

    green · in full
    A bound on a false reply being accepted, proved, against any prover, that covers the run: one reply a proof, each member of a batch checked alone, the row's parameters, its conditions shown to hold for the row's files, and the verifier program the row runs proved or stated to implement what the bound is about. Every exponent in the stated bound 128 or more; a term 2^-t or q/2^t has exponent t.
    amber · in part
    The same, with every exponent 100 or more.
    amber · in lesser part
    A bound that is conjectured or whose basis is not said, with every exponent 100 or more. Or a proved bound that does not cover the run: the adversary not said; stated per part, per round, or with no unit said; more than one reply a proof, or replies a proof not recorded; stated only for batch members handed in together, or not said for batch members, or the batch size not recorded; the parameters not the row's or not said; the conditions not shown for the row's files; the verifier program the row runs not proved or stated to implement what the bound is about. Or a proved bound with an exponent under 100.
    red · no
    No bound stated, a bound against a weaker prover, an exponent under 80, a conjectured bound or one whose basis is not said with an exponent under 100, or our Model, Prompt or Reply check failed.

    Weights hidden

    green · in full
    Zero knowledge proved for the configuration the row runs, the programs stated to implement it, and a published list of what stays visible with nothing fitted to the weights.
    amber · in part
    Zero knowledge proved for the protocol only, the programs only expected, or not stated, to implement it, no list of what stays visible, or a visible value fitted to the weights.
    amber · in lesser part
    Hiding stated, with no zero-knowledge theorem.
    red · no
    The verifier receives the weights, hiding is stated not to hold or not stated, or our Verify without weights check failed.

    Faithful model

    green · in full
    The proved replies are shown above the midpoint between a second run of the publisher's model and its Q8_0 copy, in a fidelity run of one request at a time.
    amber · in part
    Shown above the midpoint between the Q8_0 and Q4_K_M copies. Replies also shown above the green midpoint, in a fidelity run of more than one request at a time, stay here.
    amber · in lesser part
    Fidelity held but the replies are not shown above the Q8_0 and Q4_K_M midpoint; the claim accepts tokens other than the model's top token; or the second run, the Q8_0 copy or the Q4_K_M copy is missing or failed, or no step tests are stored.
    red · no
    Our Fidelity check failed.

    Machine-checked

    green · in full
    A machine-checked soundness theorem covers the verifier program the row runs, from the bytes it reads to its verdict, with its trusted base listed and no input code outside it, and a build of it at the pinned commit, with the toolchain the publisher pins, is recorded.
    amber · in part
    Machine-checked for the whole protocol and for the statement that encodes the model, but not for the verifier program the row runs, or for that program without a listed trusted base, or with input code outside the proof or not stated to be inside it.
    amber · in lesser part
    Machine-checked for a part, or for the protocol or the model's statement alone.
    red · no
    Nothing machine-checked or not stated, a recorded build that failed or reports axioms other than those the publisher declares, or our Model, Prompt or Reply check failed where it was green.

    No third party trusted

    green · in full
    The publisher's documents show soundness resting on cryptographic assumptions alone: no setup ever produced a secret. Public files fixed before the hash, or derived from public inputs, do not count.
    amber · in part
    A setup produced a secret in an open ceremony with a public transcript; the secret stays unknown if one participant was honest.
    amber · in lesser part
    One honest party among a fixed set, a challenger in a challenge window, or proofs only a designated party can check or checked interactively.
    red · no
    A setup run by one party, a hardware maker's key, a trusted key, or not stated.

    A petal is hollow while it is undecided: its properties file is not read yet, its fidelity run is not measured or still undecided, or, for Machine-checked, no build of the named proof with the toolchain the publisher pins is recorded yet. Silence is not hollow: a property the publisher does not state is red, drawn as an outline in the red ink, its name not struck.

    not stated
    Red: the publisher's documents at the pinned commit do not state the property. Drawn as an outline in the red ink, its name not struck.
    stated, short of amber
    Red: the publisher states that the property does not hold, or the facts it states fall short of amber.
    our check failed
    Red: one of our checks failed, or a recorded build of the publisher's machine-checked proof failed or reported other axioms. The petal names which.
    not read yet
    Hollow: the publisher's documents are not read yet for the commit the row runs.
    our check undecided
    Hollow: the petal is measured, and its check is undecided or has no record yet.
    no proof build yet
    Hollow: the publisher states a reach above red for Machine-checked, and no build of the named proof at the pinned commit, with the toolchain the publisher pins, is recorded yet.

    Our reading: a fact the petal's step rests on is our reading of the publisher's quoted words, not a sentence that states it. The quotes are printed with the petal.

    The six checks

    The six checks are tests we run on every system. A system passes on a model when all six checks are held there. The five petals grade what the publisher states, and Faithful model what our fidelity run measured: a failed check turns its petals red, a held check never turns one green.

    These checks run each system's own prover and verifier. None attempts a forged proof: soundness and weight secrecy are not tested.

    Run

    held
    Each of 3 sessions, from a cold start on its own prompts, returned an accepted proof for every request, built from the published code at its pinned commit, unpatched, with no flag that weakens the proof.
    failed
    One set of prompts did not complete in 5 attempts, the system's own code refused the request, or a counted session used a flag that weakens the proof.

    Model

    held
    A proof made after one weight was changed is not accepted, and an honest proof checked against the changed weights' commitment is refused, in 3 of 3 trials each.
    failed
    Any trial is accepted: the verifier accepts a proof for weights other than the committed ones.

    Prompt

    held
    A proof checked with one prompt token changed is refused, in 3 of 3 trials.
    failed
    Any trial is accepted: the verifier accepts a proof for a prompt other than the one proved.

    Reply

    held
    Every accepted proof covers all 512 reply tokens, and a changed reply token is refused at the first, the last and one interior position drawn from a public seed, in 3 of 3 trials each.
    failed
    A proof covers fewer reply tokens, or any trial is accepted: the verifier accepts a reply with a token changed.

    Verify without weights

    held
    Every counted verdict came from a verify call with no GPU visible, on a machine holding no model file, and the trace of the files it opened names no model file.
    failed
    The trace names a model file, or a verify call needed one.

    Fidelity

    held
    The system's replies match the replies of the publisher's original weights for more leading tokens than the bar's do. The bar is the copy we damaged on purpose that matches most, among those every honest copy scores above. A floor, not a measure of closeness: the Faithful model petal grades closeness.
    failed
    The bar scores above the system's replies: they match the replies of the publisher's original weights for fewer leading tokens than those of a copy damaged on purpose.

    Undecided

    system limit
    the system cannot do what the test needs; the publisher's sentence is quoted
    not run
    the test has not been run
    not resolved
    the test ran and decided nothing
    not reproduced
    a verifier accepted a changed input in our adapter; not yet reproduced with the system's own command

    Figures

    Cost vs serving

    proving
    m, GPU-seconds per proved reply after the first group. In one session, proofs released within 1 s of the previous release form a group. m is the GPU time from the first group's last release to the session's last release, over the replies of the later groups, generating the replies included. The mean of 3 sessions, each from a cold start.
    serving
    b, GPU-seconds per reply of plain serving: the publisher's own release, served by the pinned engine on the fewest H200 that hold it, at full load. Full load is the lowest concurrency that reaches 95 % of the highest rate, on a ladder up to 1,024 concurrent requests.
    multiple
    m over b, on one model. Both halves are GPU-seconds of the same GPU at one price, so the multiple is the same in dollars.
    whole session
    A system whose sessions release too few groups, or uneven ones, has no m. It prints its session cost per reply over b, labelled as whole sessions.
    parts
    Where a system generates its replies in a phase of its own, the figure is printed in two parts, each over b: generation, that phase's GPU time over the replies, and the rest. Under m the rest is proving; under whole sessions it is commit and proving.

    Cost per reply

    steady
    m in USD, at the release's price of one H200 GPU-hour.
    session
    Where a system has no multiple: the GPU time from the start of commit to the release of the last proof, over the session's replies, in USD.

    Verify time per reply

    verify
    The time the system's verifier takes on a session's proofs, over the session's replies.

    KL to the original weights

    kl
    The mean KL divergence per scored token, in nats, of the file the system commits from the reference: the publisher's original weights, 16-bit for Llama 4 Maverick 17B-128E Instruct and Llama 3.1 8B Instruct, 32-bit for GPT-2.
    computed by
    llama.cpp, on the committed file, not on the proved computation. It is printed and decides no check.
    none
    Not measured where llama.cpp cannot load the committed file.

    Order

    ranked
    Only a system that passed on a model, and only on that model.
    above
    A system is placed above another when its multiple is at least 10 % lower and its highest session m is below the other's lowest. A system with a multiple is placed above one without. Two without compare the same way on session cost per reply.
    position
    1 plus the number of systems placed above. The count of systems run on the model is printed beside it.
    unranked
    A system that passed is ranked only when its publisher states a security level at the pinned commit.
    glyph
    The glyph never moves a position.
    models
    Nothing is ranked across models.
